Prompt Chaining Architecture & FAQ

Why is a 3-stage chain better than one prompt?

When asked to draft and critique simultaneously, LLMs suffer from "confirmation bias" and skip deep self-correction. Forcing Stage 1 (Deconstruction) → Stage 2 (Draft) → Stage 3 (Red-Team Critique) yields drastically higher rigor.

Can I modify the prompt templates?

Yes. Click "View & Customize 3-Stage Prompt Templates" to tailor the instructions or introduce specific domain rubrics, coding standards, or tone constraints.

Is this secure for confidential business briefs?

Yes. The entire sequential flow happens in your browser's JavaScript runtime. Your prompts never transit any third-party analytics or intermediary servers.

What happens if an API call fails mid-chain?

The studio displays an inline alert indicating which stage encountered an error (e.g. rate limit or invalid key) so you can fix settings without losing prior completed stages.
